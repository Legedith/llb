ABOUT

What this index claims

This is a structured starting map of the DU LL.B. course-material catalog. It distinguishes source order from prerequisite order and preserves edition warnings. The Markdown files are enrichment scaffolds, not completed legal notes.

The strict graph is validated as acyclic. Related legal ideas may still point to each other because law is a network, not a simple textbook list.
