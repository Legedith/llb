1958 · Supreme Court of India · AIR 1958 SC 560

State of Madras v Gannon Dunkerley & Co

The Court held that the traditional sale concept required an agreement to sell goods and transfer of property as goods; Parliament later altered the constitutional framework for works contracts.

It is a classic example of sale classification, legislative response and constitutional amendment.

1951 · Supreme Court of India · AIR 1951 SC 144

Pannalal Jankidas v Mohanlal

The agent was liable for the loss flowing from failure to insure, illustrating causation and the agent's duty to follow lawful instructions with reasonable skill.

It is a practical Indian agency and damages case.

1860 · House of Lords (United Kingdom) · (1860) 8 HL Cas 268

Cox v Hickman

The House of Lords rejected profit sharing as a conclusive test and focused on whether the business was carried on on behalf of the alleged partners through a relationship of agency.

It is the classical source of the real-relation and mutual-agency tests used in Indian partnership law.
