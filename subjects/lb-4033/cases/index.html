2018 · Supreme Court of India · [2018] 14 SCR 489

Competition Commission of India v Bharti Airtel Ltd

The Court required foundational technical issues within TRAI's domain to be determined first, after which the CCI could examine competition-law consequences.

It is the principal authority on regulator sequencing and institutional comity.

2017 · Supreme Court of India · [2017] 5 SCR 901

Excel Crop Care Ltd v Competition Commission of India

The Court sustained the cartel findings and held that penalty should ordinarily be calculated on relevant turnover rather than total enterprise turnover.

It is a core Indian cartel and penalty case.

2010 · Supreme Court of India · (2010) 10 SCC 744

Competition Commission of India v Steel Authority of India Ltd

The Court treated the prima facie investigation direction as administrative and non-appealable at that stage, while explaining CCI procedure and later hearing rights.

It is the foundational institutional-procedure case under the Competition Act.
