Pooja Ramesh Singh v Jammu and Kashmir Bank Ltd

The Court set aside the tainted decisions, held that a decision infected by fabricated precedent cannot stand, adopted zero tolerance for unverified hallucinated authority and directed the Bar Council of India to consider professional standards.

It is a crucial current case for legal research, citation verification, judicial reasoning and professional responsibility.

Ex-Capt Harish Uppal v Union of India

The Court held that lawyers have no right to strike or give a call for boycott, subject only to an extremely narrow symbolic protest in the rarest circumstances.
