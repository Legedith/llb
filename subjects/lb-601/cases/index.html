2026 · Supreme Court of India · 2026 INSC 668

Pooja Ramesh Singh v Jammu and Kashmir Bank Ltd

The Court set aside the tainted decisions, held that a decision infected by fabricated precedent cannot stand, adopted zero tolerance for unverified hallucinated authority and directed the Bar Council of India to consider professional standards.

It is a crucial current case for legal research, citation verification, judicial reasoning and professional responsibility.

2009 · Supreme Court of India · (2009) 8 SCC 106

R K Anand v Registrar, Delhi High Court

The Court upheld contempt findings, examined professional misconduct and stressed the duty of advocates to preserve trial integrity.

It is a vivid case study in witness tampering, media evidence, contempt and professional ethics.

2008 · Supreme Court of India · (2008) 1 SCC 1

Eastern Book Company v D B Modak

The Court rejected pure sweat of the brow and required creativity in the selection or arrangement, while recognising protection for qualifying editorial contributions but not the underlying judgments.

It is the central Indian originality case and is especially relevant to a case catalogue's copyright discipline.

2002 · Supreme Court of India · (2003) 2 SCC 45

Ex-Capt Harish Uppal v Union of India

The Court held that lawyers have no right to strike or give a call for boycott, subject only to an extremely narrow symbolic protest in the rarest circumstances.

It is the controlling professional-ethics authority on strikes and court duty.

2002 · Supreme Court of India · Citation to verify

Rupa Ashok Hurra v Ashok Hurra

To prevent a gross miscarriage of justice in exceptional circumstances, the Court recognised a tightly confined curative petition following review, subject to certification, circulation and demanding threshold conditions.

It is the foundation of curative jurisdiction and finality safeguards.

2000 · Supreme Court of India · (2000) 7 SCC 264

R D Saxena v Balram Prasad Sharma

The Court held that an advocate has no lien over the client's case papers and must return them, subject to lawful fee remedies.

It is the principal authority on client files, withdrawal and professional duty.
