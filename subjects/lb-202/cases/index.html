2020 · Supreme Court of India · (2020) 9 SCC 1

Vineeta Sharma v Rakesh Sharma

The Court held that the right is by birth and does not depend on the father's survival on the commencement date, while protecting certain completed partitions.

It is the current leading authority on daughters' equal coparcenary rights.

2015 · Supreme Court of India · (2016) 2 SCC 36

Prakash v Phulavati

The decision adopted a prospective approach requiring the father to be alive. That proposition was later displaced by Vineeta Sharma.

It is important as an overruled step in the doctrinal trail.

1995 · Supreme Court of India · (1995) 3 SCC 693

Mahboob Sahab v Syed Ismail

The Court reiterated declaration by the donor, acceptance by the donee and delivery of possession, subject to the legal character of the property and transaction.

It is a frequently used Indian authority on the essentials of Muslim gifts.

currentDirect source link
1977 · Supreme Court of India · (1977) 3 SCC 99

V Tulasamma v Sesha Reddy

Property possessed in recognition of a pre-existing right is enlarged under section 14(1); section 14(2) is confined to a genuinely new restricted grant.

It is the foundational authority on enlargement of Hindu women's estates.

currentDirect source link
1969 · Supreme Court of India · Citation to verify

Yogendra Nath Naskar v Commissioner of Income Tax

A consecrated deity may be recognised as a juristic person capable of holding property and being represented through a human manager, with legal personality serving the purposes of the endowment.

It is a foundational Indian example of functional juristic personality.
