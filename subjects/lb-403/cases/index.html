2008 · Supreme Court of India · (2008) 3 SCC 1

Anuj Garg v Hotel Association of India

The Court rejected paternalistic exclusion and required the State to address real safety risks through less discriminatory means rather than deny women agency and employment.

It is a leading anti-stereotyping and proportionality case in gender equality.

2004 · Supreme Court of India · (2004) 3 SCC 514

Workmen of Nilgiri Cooperative Marketing Society Ltd v State of Tamil Nadu

The Court applied a multi-factor inquiry including control, organisation, integration, ownership of tools, method of payment and the parties' real relationship.

It is a modern synthesis of employment-status tests.

2001 · Supreme Court of India · (2001) 7 SCC 1

Steel Authority of India Ltd v National Union Waterfront Workers

The Court rejected automatic absorption and overruled the contrary Air India approach, while preserving adjudication of sham contracts and genuine employment relationships.

It is the controlling authority on contract labour, absorption and sham arrangements.

2000 · Supreme Court of India · (2000) 3 SCC 224

Municipal Corporation of Delhi v Female Workers (Muster Roll)

The Court extended maternity benefit on constitutional, statutory and social-justice grounds, rejecting status-based denial.

It is a leading labour and gender-equality authority on care work and informal employment.

Punjab Land Development and Reclamation Corporation Ltd v Presiding Officer, Labour Court

1978 · Supreme Court of India · (1978) 2 SCC 213

Bangalore Water Supply and Sewerage Board v A Rajappa

The Court formulated the triple test of systematic activity, cooperation between employer and employee and production or distribution of goods or services for human wants, subject to limited sovereign-function exclusion.

It remains the foundational industry-definition case, though later legislation and commencement questions must be checked.

Excel Wear v Union of India

1976 · Supreme Court of India · (1976) 1 SCC 822

State Bank of India v N Sundara Money

The Court adopted a broad reading covering termination by the employer for any reason other than statutory exclusions.

It is a foundational retrenchment-definition case, later shaped by legislation and larger-bench decisions.

1973 · Supreme Court of India · (1973) 1 SCC 813

Workmen of Firestone Tyre & Rubber Co of India v Management

The Court explained when a tribunal may reappraise evidence, permit the employer to prove misconduct and examine punishment, principles later reflected in section 11A.

It is the foundational map for domestic inquiry and dismissal review.

1963 · Supreme Court of India · Citation to verify

Sur Enamel and Stamping Works Ltd v Workmen

The worker must receive clear notice of the charge, a real opportunity to answer and test adverse material, and a decision by an unbiased authority on evidence placed in the inquiry.

It gives a compact natural-justice checklist for workplace discipline.

1958 · Supreme Court of India · Citation to verify

Workmen of Dimakuchi Tea Estate v Management of Dimakuchi Tea Estate

The dispute must have a real or substantial nexus with employment, non-employment, terms of employment or labour conditions and with the collective interest of workmen; a remote personal controversy is insufficient.

It is a foundational case on the scope and parties of an industrial dispute.
