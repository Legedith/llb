Nathi Devi v Radha Devi Gupta

Citation requires verification

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

The meaning of a rent-control provision depended on reading its words within the enactment as a whole.

Issue

May a court depart from clear statutory language in the name of purpose?

Holding

Where language is clear and produces a workable result, the court applies it; purpose and context assist interpretation but do not authorise addition or subtraction of words.

Why it matters

It is useful for literal meaning, whole-statute reading and the boundary of judicial construction.
