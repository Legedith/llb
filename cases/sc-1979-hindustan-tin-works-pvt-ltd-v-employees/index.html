Hindustan Tin Works Pvt Ltd v Employees

Citation requires verification

limitedDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

Employees were illegally terminated and the dispute concerned reinstatement and the measure of back wages.

Issue

What principles govern back wages after wrongful termination?

Holding

Reinstatement may ordinarily carry restoration of lost wages, but relief depends on the facts, including the nature of illegality, delay, gainful employment and equitable considerations; later law has made the inquiry more contextual.

Why it matters

It is an origin point for the remedial debate over reinstatement and back wages.
