Toyota Jidosha Kabushiki Kaisha v Prius Auto Industries Ltd

(2018) 2 SCC 1

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

Toyota challenged Indian use of the PRIUS mark and relied on global reputation predating significant Indian market presence.

Issue

How much territorial goodwill must a foreign mark owner prove for passing off in India?

Holding

The Court required evidence that the mark's reputation had penetrated the Indian market at the relevant time and refused to assume worldwide fame automatically established local goodwill.

Why it matters

It is the modern territorial qualification on transborder-reputation claims.
