Supreme Court of India · 1985

Gian Devi Anand v Jeevan Kumar

Citation requires verification

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

After a tenant's death, the issue was whether a commercial tenancy and statutory protection passed to legal heirs.

Issue

Is a commercial tenancy heritable under general rent-control principles?

Holding

In the absence of a contrary statutory provision, tenancy rights and statutory protection are generally heritable; residential and commercial tenancies cannot be differentiated merely by judicial assumption.

Why it matters

It is the leading authority on heritability of commercial tenancy.
