Supreme Court of India · 1981

K P Varghese v Income Tax Officer

(1981) 4 SCC 173

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

The revenue sought to tax an assumed understatement of sale consideration based on market value without proving that the seller actually received more.

Issue

Whether a provision aimed at concealed consideration applies merely because market value exceeds the stated price.

Holding

The Court used text, statutory purpose and legislative history to require proof of understatement rather than taxing a fictional receipt.

Why it matters

It is both a tax case and a leading authority on purposive interpretation and use of external aids.
