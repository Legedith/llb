Supreme Court of India · 2003

Union of India v Azadi Bachao Andolan

(2004) 10 SCC 1

current with legislationDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

A circular and the use of the India–Mauritius tax treaty by foreign investors were challenged as enabling treaty shopping and avoidance.

Issue

Whether treaty-based tax planning and the government's circular were unlawful merely because they reduced tax.

Holding

The Court upheld the circular and rejected the proposition that every tax-avoidance arrangement is illegitimate; legal form and treaty entitlement matter unless the law supplies a contrary rule.

Why it matters

It is the principal corrective to overbroad readings of McDowell.
