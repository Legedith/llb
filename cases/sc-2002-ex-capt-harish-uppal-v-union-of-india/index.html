Supreme Court of India · 2002

Ex-Capt Harish Uppal v Union of India

(2003) 2 SCC 45

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

Repeated lawyer strikes and boycotts disrupted court work and litigants' access to justice.

Issue

Do advocates have a right to strike or boycott courts?

Holding

The Court held that lawyers have no right to strike or give a call for boycott, subject only to an extremely narrow symbolic protest in the rarest circumstances.

Why it matters

It is the controlling professional-ethics authority on strikes and court duty.
