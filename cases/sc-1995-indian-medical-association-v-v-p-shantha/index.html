Supreme Court of India · 1995

Indian Medical Association v V P Shantha

(1995) 6 SCC 651

current with legislationDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

Doctors and medical bodies challenged the application of consumer-protection law to medical services.

Issue

Are medical services covered as 'service' under consumer legislation?

Holding

Medical services for consideration generally fall within consumer jurisdiction, subject to exclusions for wholly free service and contracts of personal service.

Why it matters

It opened a major remedial route for patients and remains important under the 2019 Act.
