N R Dongre v Whirlpool Corporation

(1996) 5 SCC 714

limited by territoriality emphasisDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

A foreign mark owner sought to restrain Indian use despite limited domestic sales, relying on international reputation and advertising spillover.

Issue

Can transborder reputation support passing off in India?

Holding

The Court upheld interim protection on evidence that the mark's reputation had reached India and that local use risked deception.

Why it matters

It is a leading transborder-reputation authority, later refined by territoriality cases.
