Supreme Court of India · 2020

Tofan Singh v State of Tamil Nadu

(2021) 4 SCC 1

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

Convictions under the NDPS Act relied on confessional statements made to officers exercising investigative powers.

Issue

Are empowered NDPS officers 'police officers' for the confession exclusion, and can statements to them be used as confessions?

Holding

The majority held that such officers are police officers for the relevant evidentiary rule and that confessional statements to them are inadmissible as confessions.

Why it matters

It is the leading authority on NDPS confessions and the relationship between special investigation and evidence law.
