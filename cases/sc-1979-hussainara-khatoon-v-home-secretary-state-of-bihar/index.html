Hussainara Khatoon v Home Secretary, State of Bihar

(1980) 1 SCC 81

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

Public-interest petitions revealed that undertrial prisoners had remained incarcerated for periods longer than the possible sentences for their alleged offences.

Issue

Does Article 21 include a right to speedy trial and legal assistance for indigent accused persons?

Holding

The Court treated speedy trial and meaningful legal aid as components of fair procedure and ordered release and systemic action.

Why it matters

It is the foundational Indian case on undertrial justice and speedy trial.
