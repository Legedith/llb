Supreme Court of India · 1985

McDowell and Co Ltd v Commercial Tax Officer

(1985) 3 SCC 230

limitedDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

A company structured payment of excise duty in a way that reduced the turnover figure used for sales tax.

Issue

How should courts distinguish legitimate tax planning from colourable devices?

Holding

The judgments condemned colourable devices, but later authority clarified that legitimate tax planning remains permissible and that McDowell should not be read as a universal anti-avoidance rule.

Why it matters

It is important chiefly when read together with Azadi Bachao Andolan and Vodafone.
