Supreme Court of India · 1992

State of Haryana v Bhajan Lal

1992 Supp (1) SCC 335

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

Criminal proceedings based on allegations against a former Chief Minister raised questions about investigation and High Court interference.

Issue

When may a High Court quash an FIR or criminal proceeding at the threshold?

Holding

The Court identified illustrative categories where proceedings may be quashed, while warning that the power is exceptional and facts ordinarily require investigation.

Why it matters

It is the standard framework for inherent and writ quashing.
