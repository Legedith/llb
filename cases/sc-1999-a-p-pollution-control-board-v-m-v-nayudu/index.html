Supreme Court of India · 1999

A P Pollution Control Board v M V Nayudu

(1999) 2 SCC 718

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

An industrial project near drinking-water reservoirs raised scientific uncertainty and institutional competence concerns.

Issue

How should courts and regulators deal with uncertain environmental risk and technical evidence?

Holding

The Court emphasised precaution, the limits of conventional adjudication and the need for expert environmental decision-making.

Why it matters

It is an anchor for precaution under uncertainty, expert bodies and environmental evidence.
