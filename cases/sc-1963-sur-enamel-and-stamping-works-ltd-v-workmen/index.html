Supreme Court of India · 1963

Sur Enamel and Stamping Works Ltd v Workmen

Citation requires verification

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

A dismissal followed a domestic inquiry whose fairness and evidentiary process were challenged.

Issue

What minimum features make a domestic disciplinary inquiry fair?

Holding

The worker must receive clear notice of the charge, a real opportunity to answer and test adverse material, and a decision by an unbiased authority on evidence placed in the inquiry.

Why it matters

It gives a compact natural-justice checklist for workplace discipline.
