Renusagar Power Co Ltd v General Electric Co

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

Enforcement of a foreign arbitral award was opposed on public-policy grounds.

Issue

How narrowly should public policy be understood at the foreign-award enforcement stage?

Holding

For foreign awards, public policy was confined to fundamental policy, the interests of India and justice or morality, rather than a merits review. Later legislation and cases refined the language but preserved a narrow enforcement orientation.

Why it matters

It is the starting point for India's pro-enforcement public-policy doctrine.
