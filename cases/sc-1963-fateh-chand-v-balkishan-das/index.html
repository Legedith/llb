Supreme Court of India · 1963

Fateh Chand v Balkishan Das

AIR 1963 SC 1405

current with later casesDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

A seller forfeited amounts paid under a property agreement after the purchaser failed to complete the transaction.

Issue

How does section 74 govern stipulated sums and forfeiture after breach?

Holding

Section 74 awards reasonable compensation up to the named amount; it does not permit automatic recovery or forfeiture disconnected from breach and legally cognisable loss.

Why it matters

It is the leading foundation for penalty clauses and reasonable compensation.
