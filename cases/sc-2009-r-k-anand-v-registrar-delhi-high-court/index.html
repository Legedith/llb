Supreme Court of India · 2009

R K Anand v Registrar, Delhi High Court

(2009) 8 SCC 106

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

A televised sting showed senior advocates attempting to influence a prosecution witness in the BMW hit-and-run trial.

Issue

How should courts respond to advocate conduct that interferes with witnesses and the administration of justice?

Holding

The Court upheld contempt findings, examined professional misconduct and stressed the duty of advocates to preserve trial integrity.

Why it matters

It is a vivid case study in witness tampering, media evidence, contempt and professional ethics.
