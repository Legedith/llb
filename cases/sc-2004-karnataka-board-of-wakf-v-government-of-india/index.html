Supreme Court of India · 2004

Karnataka Board of Wakf v Government of India

Citation requires verification

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

A claimant asserted title by adverse possession against the recorded owner.

Issue

What must a party plead and prove to establish adverse possession?

Holding

Possession must be actual, open, continuous, exclusive and hostile to the true owner's title for the statutory period; the claimant must identify when and how hostility began and cannot rely on vague long possession alone.

Why it matters

It supplies the pleading and proof checklist for adverse possession.
