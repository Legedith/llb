Eastern Book Company v D B Modak

(2008) 1 SCC 1

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

Publishers claimed copyright in edited versions of Supreme Court judgments, including headnotes and editorial enhancements.

Issue

What level of originality is required for copyright in compilations and editorial work?

Holding

The Court rejected pure sweat of the brow and required creativity in the selection or arrangement, while recognising protection for qualifying editorial contributions but not the underlying judgments.

Why it matters

It is the central Indian originality case and is especially relevant to a case catalogue's copyright discipline.
