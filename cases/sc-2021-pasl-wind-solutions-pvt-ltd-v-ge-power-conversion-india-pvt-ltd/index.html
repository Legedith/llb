PASL Wind Solutions Pvt Ltd v GE Power Conversion India Pvt Ltd

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

Two Indian parties selected a foreign seat for arbitration and later litigated enforcement and interim-relief questions in India.

Issue

May Indian parties choose a foreign arbitral seat, and how is the resulting award treated?

Holding

Party autonomy permits Indian parties to choose a foreign seat absent a specific statutory prohibition; an award made there can be a foreign award, while mandatory Indian law may still affect the underlying transaction and available relief.

Why it matters

It connects seat theory, party autonomy, enforcement and domestic mandatory law.
