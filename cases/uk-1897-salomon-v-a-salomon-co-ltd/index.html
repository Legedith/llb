House of Lords (United Kingdom) · 1897

Salomon v A Salomon & Co Ltd

[1897] AC 22

currentDiscovery link — verify
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

A sole trader incorporated a company and became its secured creditor; after insolvency, unsecured creditors argued that the company was merely his agent.

Issue

Whether a duly incorporated company is legally separate from its controlling shareholder.

Holding

The company was a separate legal person with its own rights and liabilities, notwithstanding concentrated ownership.

Why it matters

It is the foundational comparative authority for separate corporate personality and limited liability.
