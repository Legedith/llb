Supreme Court of India · 2004

United India Insurance Co Ltd v Harchand Rai Chandan Lal

Citation requires verification

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

An insured claimed indemnity for a loss and disputed the insurer's reliance on the policy's coverage language and exclusions.

Issue

How should an insurance policy's coverage and exclusions be construed?

Holding

The court must give effect to the policy as written, reading coverage and exclusions together; sympathy cannot create a risk the parties did not insure, although ambiguity and consumer-law principles may affect other cases.

Why it matters

It is a central reminder that insurance liability begins with the precise insured peril.
