Supreme Court of India · 2000

R D Saxena v Balram Prasad Sharma

(2000) 7 SCC 264

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

An advocate retained a former client's litigation file to secure unpaid fees.

Issue

Does an advocate have a lien over litigation files and may the file be withheld?

Holding

The Court held that an advocate has no lien over the client's case papers and must return them, subject to lawful fee remedies.

Why it matters

It is the principal authority on client files, withdrawal and professional duty.
