International Court of Justice · 1970

Barcelona Traction, Light and Power Company, Limited

ICJ Reports 1970, p 3

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

Belgium sought to protect shareholders in a Canadian company harmed by measures taken in Spain.

Issue

Which State may exercise diplomatic protection for a corporation, and what are obligations erga omnes?

Holding

The Court generally linked corporate diplomatic protection to the State of incorporation and distinguished obligations owed to the international community as a whole.

Why it matters

It is foundational for diplomatic protection, corporate nationality and obligations erga omnes.
