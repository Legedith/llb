Modi Entertainment Network v WSG Cricket Pte Ltd

Citation requires verification

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

Parallel proceedings and a contractual foreign forum led to a request that an Indian court restrain litigation abroad.

Issue

When may an Indian court grant an anti-suit injunction?

Holding

An anti-suit injunction is an equitable order against a party, not the foreign court. It requires personal jurisdiction, caution, comity, prevention of injustice and a strong case that the chosen foreign proceeding is oppressive, vexatious or contrary to a binding forum arrangement.

Why it matters

It is the principal Indian framework for anti-suit injunctions and forum clauses.
