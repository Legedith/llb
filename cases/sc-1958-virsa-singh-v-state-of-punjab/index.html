Supreme Court of India · 1958

Virsa Singh v State of Punjab

AIR 1958 SC 465

current with code crosswalkDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

The accused inflicted a spear injury that was sufficient in the ordinary course of nature to cause death.

Issue

What must the prosecution prove for the clause of murder dealing with an intentionally inflicted injury sufficient to cause death?

Holding

The prosecution must prove the bodily injury, that it was intentionally inflicted, and that the injury objectively was sufficient in the ordinary course of nature to cause death; intent to cause death is not separately required under that clause.

Why it matters

It is the classic element-by-element explanation of murder based on intended injury.
