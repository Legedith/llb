F Hoffmann-La Roche Ltd v Cipla Ltd

2015 SCC OnLine Del 13619

current with later casesDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

Patent litigation over the cancer drug erlotinib required claim construction, infringement analysis and validity challenges.

Issue

How should pharmaceutical patent claims be construed and compared with the accused product?

Holding

The appellate judgment applied purposive claim construction and analysed infringement separately from validity, while addressing obviousness and public-interest arguments.

Why it matters

It is a leading Indian patent-infringement case study.
