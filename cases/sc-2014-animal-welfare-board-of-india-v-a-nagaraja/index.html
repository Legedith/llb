Animal Welfare Board of India v A Nagaraja

Citation requires verification

limitedDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

The legality of animal performances and practices involving bulls was tested against animal-welfare legislation and constitutional values.

Issue

What legal interests and duties arise in relation to animal welfare?

Holding

Animal-welfare statutes must be applied to prevent unnecessary pain and protect animal well-being; constitutional duties and an ecocentric approach may inform interpretation, although the precise constitutional status of animal rights remains doctrinally contested and later legislation must be checked.

Why it matters

It is a major bridge between legal personhood debates, statutory welfare and constitutional duties.
