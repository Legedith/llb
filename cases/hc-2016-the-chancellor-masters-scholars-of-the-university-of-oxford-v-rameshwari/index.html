The Chancellor, Masters & Scholars of the University of Oxford v Rameshwari Photocopy Services

2016 SCC OnLine Del 6780

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

Academic publishers sued a university photocopy shop producing course packs of extracts prescribed by teachers.

Issue

Does preparation and distribution of course packs for instruction fall within the education exception to copyright?

Holding

The Division Bench adopted a broad reading of use in the course of instruction and rejected infringement on the facts, while emphasising the educational setting.

Why it matters

It is a major Indian reading-material case directly relevant to lawful course enrichment.
