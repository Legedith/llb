Supreme Court of India · 1966

Nathulal v State of Madhya Pradesh

AIR 1966 SC 43

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

A grain dealer believed that his licence application would be granted and was prosecuted for possession without a licence.

Issue

Is mens rea presumed where a regulatory offence is silent about fault?

Holding

The Court applied the presumption of mens rea and acquitted because the accused acted under an honest belief inconsistent with the required guilty mind.

Why it matters

It is the standard counterpoint to Mayer Hans George.
