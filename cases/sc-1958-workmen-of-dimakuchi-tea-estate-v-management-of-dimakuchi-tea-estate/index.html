Supreme Court of India · 1958

Workmen of Dimakuchi Tea Estate v Management of Dimakuchi Tea Estate

Citation requires verification

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

A dispute concerned a person whose relationship to the workmen and employment setting was contested.

Issue

What connection must a dispute have with workmen to qualify as an industrial dispute?

Holding

The dispute must have a real or substantial nexus with employment, non-employment, terms of employment or labour conditions and with the collective interest of workmen; a remote personal controversy is insufficient.

Why it matters

It is a foundational case on the scope and parties of an industrial dispute.
