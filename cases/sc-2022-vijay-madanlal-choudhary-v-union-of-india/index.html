Vijay Madanlal Choudhary v Union of India

2022 SCC OnLine SC 929

current with later qualificationsDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

Numerous petitioners challenged core provisions of the Prevention of Money Laundering Act governing offence structure, attachment, summons, arrest, burden and bail.

Issue

Are the PMLA's enforcement and bail provisions constitutionally valid, and how is the money-laundering offence structured?

Holding

The Court substantially upheld the framework and adopted a broad reading of the continuing process or activity connected with proceeds of crime, while certain later cases have imposed procedural safeguards.

Why it matters

It is the primary structural authority on PMLA, but every proposition must be checked against later judgments and legislative changes.
