Supreme Court of India · 1972

Bennett Coleman & Co v Union of India

(1972) 2 SCC 788

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

Newspaper companies challenged newsprint policy restrictions that limited page growth and circulation.

Issue

Can economic regulation of newsprint violate press freedom through its direct effect?

Holding

The Court held that the policy's direct effect on pages, circulation and newspaper growth infringed freedom of the press.

Why it matters

It is a leading authority on indirect burdens, corporate press claims and effects-based review.
