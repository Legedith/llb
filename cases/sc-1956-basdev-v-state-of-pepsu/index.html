Supreme Court of India · 1956

Basdev v State of Pepsu

AIR 1956 SC 488

current with code crosswalkDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

An intoxicated accused shot a young boy at a wedding and relied on drunkenness.

Issue

When does intoxication negate criminal intent or knowledge?

Holding

Voluntary intoxication generally does not excuse; knowledge may be imputed as if sober, while the prosecution must still prove any specific intention required by the offence.

Why it matters

It is the standard Indian authority on voluntary intoxication.
