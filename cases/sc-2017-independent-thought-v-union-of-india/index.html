Supreme Court of India · 2017

Independent Thought v Union of India

(2017) 10 SCC 800

current with code crosswalkDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

A child-rights organisation challenged the marital-rape exception insofar as it covered wives between fifteen and eighteen years old.

Issue

Can the marital exception protect sexual intercourse with a wife who is legally a child?

Holding

The Court read down the exception so that intercourse with a wife below eighteen falls within the rape offence, harmonising criminal, child-protection and marriage laws.

Why it matters

It is central to child marriage, marital status, consent and harmonious statutory interpretation.
