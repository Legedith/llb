Supreme Court of India · 1986

Life Insurance Corporation of India v Escorts Ltd

(1986) 1 SCC 264

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

Foreign investment and corporate shareholding disputes led to arguments that the Court should disregard corporate form and examine the real actors behind the companies.

Issue

When may courts lift the corporate veil?

Holding

The Court recognised that veil lifting is exceptional and depends on statute, fraud, evasion, agency or the demands of the particular legal rule; it is not a general licence to ignore incorporation.

Why it matters

It is a leading Indian statement of veil-piercing limits.
