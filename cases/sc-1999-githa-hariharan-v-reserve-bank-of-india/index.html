Supreme Court of India · 1999

Githa Hariharan v Reserve Bank of India

[1999] 1 SCR 669

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

A mother challenged a rule and statutory understanding that treated the father as natural guardian 'after' whom the mother could act.

Issue

Does the guardianship statute subordinate the mother while the father is alive?

Holding

The Court interpreted 'after' as meaning in the father's absence from care, not only after his death, thereby avoiding a discriminatory hierarchy.

Why it matters

It is a leading gender-equal and rights-consistent interpretation of guardianship.
