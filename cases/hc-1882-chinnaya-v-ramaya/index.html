Chinnaya v Ramaya

ILR (1882) 4 Mad 137

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

A donor transferred property to her daughter subject to an obligation to make an annuity payment to another person; the daughter later resisted payment.

Issue

Whether consideration may move from a person other than the promisee under Indian contract law.

Holding

The court enforced the promise, illustrating that under the Indian Contract Act consideration may move from the promisee or another person, though privity remains a separate issue.

Why it matters

It is the standard Indian contrast with English consideration doctrine.
