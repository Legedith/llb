Supreme Court of India · 1979

V C Rangadurai v D Gopalan

(1979) 1 SCC 308

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

An advocate was found guilty of professional misconduct involving client funds and the Court considered sanction and the profession's public role.

Issue

How should disciplinary sanctions balance protection of the public, integrity of the profession and rehabilitation?

Holding

The Court stressed that advocacy is a public profession founded on trust and tailored the sanction while requiring restitution and ethical accountability.

Why it matters

It is a foundational professional-discipline and remedial case.
