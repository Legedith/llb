Supreme Court of India · 1987

Canara Bank v Canara Sales Corporation

(1987) 2 SCC 666

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

An employee forged company cheques and the bank debited the customer's account after paying them.

Issue

Who bears loss when a bank pays a cheque bearing a forged drawer signature?

Holding

A forged cheque carries no customer mandate, so the bank generally cannot debit the account unless the customer's conduct creates a legally recognised defence.

Why it matters

It is the anchor for bank mandate, forgery and duty of care.
