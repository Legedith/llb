Supreme Court of India · 2015

Kailash Nath Associates v Delhi Development Authority

[2015] 1 SCR 627

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

The highest bidder in a public auction paid earnest money; the authority later cancelled the allotment and forfeited the deposit despite extensions, lack of a clear final demand and no demonstrated loss.

Issue

Whether earnest money may be forfeited without breach and without loss under section 74.

Holding

The Court held that forfeiture requires breach and reasonable compensation; where loss is capable of proof it should be proved, and a public authority cannot retain money arbitrarily.

Why it matters

It synthesises Fateh Chand and section 74 for modern contracts and public auctions.
