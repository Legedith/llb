Supreme Court of India · 2002

Shamim Ara v State of Uttar Pradesh

(2002) 7 SCC 518

current with legislationDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

A husband relied on a plea in litigation that he had earlier divorced his wife by talaq, without proving a valid pronouncement or reconciliation process.

Issue

What is required to establish a legally effective talaq?

Holding

A mere assertion in a written statement is insufficient; talaq must be proved as pronounced for reasonable cause and preceded by an attempt at reconciliation in the framework adopted by the Court.

Why it matters

It is a major authority against unproved unilateral talaq.
