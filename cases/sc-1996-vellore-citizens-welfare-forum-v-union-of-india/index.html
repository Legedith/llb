Supreme Court of India · 1996

Vellore Citizens Welfare Forum v Union of India

[1996] Supp 5 SCR 241

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

Tanneries polluted water and agricultural land in Tamil Nadu, creating conflict between industrial activity and community health.

Issue

How do precaution, polluter-pays and sustainable development operate in Indian environmental law?

Holding

The Court treated sustainable development, precaution and polluter pays as part of Indian environmental law and ordered remedial and compensation measures.

Why it matters

It is the canonical Indian authority on core environmental principles.
