Revajeetu Builders and Developers v Narayanaswamy and Sons

Citation requires verification

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

A party sought amendment of pleadings, requiring the court to balance full adjudication against delay, prejudice and change in the nature of the case.

Issue

What factors govern amendment of pleadings?

Holding

Courts should consider whether the amendment is necessary for the real controversy, bona fide, compensable in costs, prejudicial beyond repair, limitation-sensitive or transformative of the case; the post-trial due-diligence requirement must also be respected.

Why it matters

It supplies an exam-ready factor matrix for pleading amendments.
