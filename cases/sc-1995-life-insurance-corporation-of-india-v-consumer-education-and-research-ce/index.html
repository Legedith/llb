Supreme Court of India · 1995

Life Insurance Corporation of India v Consumer Education and Research Centre

(1995) 5 SCC 482

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

Workers challenged restrictive life-insurance conditions and access to insurance products offered by a state-controlled insurer.

Issue

Whether a public insurer's standard terms and classifications are subject to constitutional fairness.

Holding

The Court treated life insurance as connected with social security and held that a public insurer's terms must satisfy fairness and non-arbitrariness.

Why it matters

It connects insurance contracts, standard terms and public-law accountability.
