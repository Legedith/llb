SEBI v Shriram Mutual Fund

Citation requires verification

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

A regulated entity breached statutory obligations and contested monetary penalty on the ground that deliberate intention was not shown.

Issue

Does a civil penalty under securities regulation always require proof of mens rea?

Holding

Where the statute creates a civil obligation and prescribed monetary consequence, contravention may attract penalty without proof of criminal mens rea, subject to the text, scheme and any statutory discretion.

Why it matters

It is a principal authority for strict civil regulatory liability.
