Supreme Court of India · 1973

His Holiness Kesavananda Bharati Sripadagalvaru v State of Kerala

[1973] Supp SCR 1

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

A religious institution challenged land reform measures and constitutional amendments that sought to protect redistributive legislation from fundamental-rights review.

Issue

How far may Parliament amend the Constitution under Article 368?

Holding

Parliament's amending power is wide but cannot damage or destroy the Constitution's basic structure. The opinions differed on detail, but the basic-structure limitation became the controlling proposition.

Why it matters

It is the central authority for limited constitutional amendment, constitutional identity and the difference between constituent power and ordinary legislative power.
