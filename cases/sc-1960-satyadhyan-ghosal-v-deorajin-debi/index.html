Satyadhyan Ghosal v Deorajin Debi

Citation requires verification

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

Successive stages of the same litigation raised whether an earlier adjudication could be reopened.

Issue

Can res judicata operate between different stages of the same proceeding?

Holding

The principle of finality can apply between stages of the same litigation so that an issue finally decided at an earlier stage is not ordinarily reopened at a later stage, subject to appellate correction and the procedural setting.

Why it matters

It connects the policy of finality to the internal life of a suit, not only to later suits.
