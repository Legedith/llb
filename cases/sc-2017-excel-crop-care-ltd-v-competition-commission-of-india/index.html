Supreme Court of India · 2017

Excel Crop Care Ltd v Competition Commission of India

[2017] 5 SCR 901

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

Companies were found to have colluded in tenders for aluminium phosphide tablets, and the dispute included the proper turnover base for penalties.

Issue

How are bid rigging, investigative scope and monetary penalties assessed under the Competition Act?

Holding

The Court sustained the cartel findings and held that penalty should ordinarily be calculated on relevant turnover rather than total enterprise turnover.

Why it matters

It is a core Indian cartel and penalty case.
