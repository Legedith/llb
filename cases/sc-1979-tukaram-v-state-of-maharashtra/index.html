Supreme Court of India · 1979

Tukaram v State of Maharashtra

(1979) 2 SCC 143

historical supersededDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

A young Adivasi girl alleged rape by police officers inside a police station; the acquittal and reasoning provoked national protest.

Issue

How should absence of physical resistance and alleged consent be treated in a custodial sexual-assault case?

Holding

The majority upheld acquittal on the then record and legal framework. The decision became a major negative precedent that triggered statutory reforms on consent, custody and evidential burdens.

Why it matters

It is historically essential for understanding rape-law reform and the dangers of equating submission with consent.
