Needle Industries (India) Ltd v Needle Industries Newey (India) Holding Ltd

(1981) 3 SCC 333

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

A dispute between controlling groups concerned a share issue and corporate conduct alleged to be oppressive.

Issue

What conduct amounts to oppression and how should equitable corporate remedies be fashioned?

Holding

Oppression requires burdensome, harsh and wrongful conduct viewed in context; the court may grant equitable relief even where technical illegality and oppression do not perfectly overlap.

Why it matters

It remains a foundational Indian authority on oppression, good faith and remedial flexibility.
