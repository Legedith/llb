Amar Nath Sehgal v Union of India

2005 (30) PTC 253 (Del)

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

A monumental mural created for a government building was removed and damaged, and the artist sued despite transfer of the physical work.

Issue

Do an author's moral rights protect integrity and reputation after transfer of the physical object?

Holding

The Court recognised strong moral-right protection against distortion, mutilation and destruction prejudicial to the artist's honour and reputation.

Why it matters

It is the leading Indian moral-rights case.
