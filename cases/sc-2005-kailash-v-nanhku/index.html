Kailash v Nanhku

Citation requires verification

limitedDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

A written statement was filed beyond the period stated in the procedural rule applicable to the proceeding.

Issue

Was the ninety-day period for filing a written statement under the then general CPC framework mandatory in every case?

Holding

The time prescription was treated as directory in the general CPC setting, with extension reserved for exceptional reasons and not routine indulgence.

Why it matters

The case teaches the difference between a directory timeline and an unlimited discretion to excuse delay.
