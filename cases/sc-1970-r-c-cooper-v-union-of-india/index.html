Supreme Court of India · 1970

R C Cooper v Union of India

(1970) 1 SCC 248

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

A shareholder challenged the nationalisation of major banks and the effect of the legislation on property and business rights.

Issue

Whether constitutional review turns on the object of state action or its direct effect on protected rights, and whether fundamental rights are isolated compartments.

Holding

The Court focused on the direct effect of the measure and rejected rigid compartmentalisation of fundamental rights.

Why it matters

It forms the bridge from A K Gopalan to Maneka Gandhi and is central to effects-based constitutional analysis.
