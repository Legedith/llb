Supreme Court of India · 1998

Spring Meadows Hospital v Harjol Ahluwalia

(1998) 4 SCC 39

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

A child suffered severe injury after a nurse administered the wrong injection; the parents also claimed compensation for their own suffering.

Issue

Who qualifies as a consumer in medical treatment and what compensation may follow grave hospital negligence?

Holding

The hospital and responsible medical personnel were liable; both the child beneficiary and parents who hired the service were recognised within the consumer framework.

Why it matters

It is a practical consumer-medical-negligence case on vicarious liability and compensation.
