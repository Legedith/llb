Supreme Court of India · 1950

A K Gopalan v State of Madras

[1950] SCR 88

overruled in partDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

A communist leader detained under preventive-detention legislation challenged the detention as violating several fundamental rights.

Issue

Whether fundamental rights operate as separate compartments and whether any legislatively prescribed procedure satisfies Article 21.

Holding

The majority treated the rights as largely separate and read 'procedure established by law' narrowly. The compartmental approach was displaced by later decisions, especially R C Cooper and Maneka Gandhi.

Why it matters

It is indispensable as a historical starting point for the evolution of Article 21 and for understanding how precedent can be narrowed or overruled.
