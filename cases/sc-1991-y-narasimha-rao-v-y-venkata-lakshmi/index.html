Y Narasimha Rao v Y Venkata Lakshmi

Citation requires verification

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

A foreign court dissolved a marriage solemnised under Indian matrimonial law on a ground and jurisdiction contested in India.

Issue

What conditions govern Indian recognition of a foreign matrimonial decree?

Holding

Recognition ordinarily requires jurisdiction and a ground of relief recognised by the matrimonial law governing the parties, a decision on merits, fair notice and opportunity, and absence of fraud or breach of Indian law.

Why it matters

It converts CPC section 13 principles into a matrimonial-recognition checklist.
