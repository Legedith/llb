Supreme Court of India · 1951

Pannalal Jankidas v Mohanlal

AIR 1951 SC 144

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

An agent failed to insure goods as instructed, and the goods were subsequently lost in circumstances that would have been covered.

Issue

What loss is recoverable where an agent breaches a duty to obtain insurance?

Holding

The agent was liable for the loss flowing from failure to insure, illustrating causation and the agent's duty to follow lawful instructions with reasonable skill.

Why it matters

It is a practical Indian agency and damages case.
