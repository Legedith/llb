Supreme Court of India · 1966

General Assurance Society Ltd v Chandmull Jain

AIR 1966 SC 1644

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

An insured claimed under a fire policy and the dispute turned on construction of the policy's terms and the insurer's cancellation rights.

Issue

How should an insurance policy be construed?

Holding

The policy must be read as a whole according to its words; courts cannot add terms, though ambiguity may be construed against the drafter in an appropriate case.

Why it matters

It is a classic Indian authority on policy construction.
