Sushila Aggarwal v State (NCT of Delhi)

(2020) 5 SCC 1

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

A Constitution Bench resolved conflicting views on whether anticipatory bail must ordinarily expire after a fixed period or at filing of the charge-sheet.

Issue

May anticipatory bail continue without an automatic time limit?

Holding

Anticipatory bail need not ordinarily be time-bound and can continue until the end of trial, though courts may impose justified conditions or duration in a particular case.

Why it matters

It is the present leading treatment of anticipatory-bail duration.
