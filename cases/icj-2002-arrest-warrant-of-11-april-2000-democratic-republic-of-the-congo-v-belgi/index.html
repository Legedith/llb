Arrest Warrant of 11 April 2000 (Democratic Republic of the Congo v Belgium)

ICJ Reports 2002, p 3

current with debateDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

Belgium issued an international arrest warrant against the serving foreign minister of Congo for alleged international crimes.

Issue

Does a serving foreign minister enjoy immunity from foreign criminal jurisdiction despite allegations of serious international crimes?

Holding

The Court held that incumbent high-ranking officials enjoy personal immunity before foreign national courts while in office, without equating immunity with impunity.

Why it matters

It is central to universal jurisdiction, immunities and the distinction between jurisdictional bar and substantive responsibility.
