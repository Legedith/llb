Military and Paramilitary Activities in and against Nicaragua (Nicaragua v United States)

ICJ Reports 1986, p 14

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

Nicaragua alleged that the United States supported armed groups, mined ports and used force contrary to international law.

Issue

How do treaty and customary rules on force, intervention and collective self-defence operate, and how is state involvement attributed?

Holding

The Court found violations of the prohibition on force and non-intervention, distinguished treaty from customary obligations and required conditions such as an armed attack and request for collective self-defence.

Why it matters

It is the central modern authority on use of force, non-intervention, custom and attribution.
