Pankaj Bansal v Union of India

2023 INSC 866

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

Persons arrested by the Enforcement Directorate were not furnished written grounds of arrest and challenged the legality of custody.

Issue

Must grounds of arrest under the PMLA be furnished in writing?

Holding

The Court required written communication of the grounds so that the arrested person can meaningfully challenge detention and seek bail.

Why it matters

It is the key procedural qualification on PMLA arrest after Vijay Madanlal.
