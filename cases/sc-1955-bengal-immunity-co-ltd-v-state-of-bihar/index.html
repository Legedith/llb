Bengal Immunity Co Ltd v State of Bihar

Citation requires verification

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

The Court interpreted constitutional tax restrictions in light of the defect addressed by the provision.

Issue

What role may the mischief rule play in constitutional or statutory interpretation?

Holding

The prior law, defect or mischief, legislative remedy and reason for the remedy may illuminate ambiguous text, while remaining anchored to the enacted words.

Why it matters

It is a major Indian use of the Heydon mischief framework.
