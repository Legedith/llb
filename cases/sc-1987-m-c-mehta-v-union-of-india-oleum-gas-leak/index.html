M C Mehta v Union of India (Oleum Gas Leak)

(1987) 1 SCC 395

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

Oleum gas escaped from a hazardous industrial unit in Delhi, causing death and injury while public-interest proceedings about the plant were pending.

Issue

What standard of liability applies to an enterprise engaged in hazardous or inherently dangerous activity?

Holding

The Court formulated absolute and non-delegable enterprise liability, without the traditional Rylands exceptions, and linked compensation to the enterprise's capacity and the magnitude of harm.

Why it matters

It is the foundational Indian authority on absolute liability, constitutional tort technique and hazardous industry.
