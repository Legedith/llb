Satya v Teja Singh

Citation requires verification

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

A spouse obtained a foreign divorce after creating a jurisdictional connection that was alleged to be artificial and fraudulent.

Issue

When should an Indian court refuse recognition to a foreign matrimonial judgment?

Holding

A foreign judgment founded on a fraudulent assumption of jurisdiction or denial of a real opportunity to contest is not entitled to recognition merely because it is formally valid in the foreign forum.

Why it matters

It is a foundational Indian conflict-of-laws case on fraud, domicile and matrimonial recognition.
