Allahabad High Court · 1913

Lalman Shukla v Gauri Dutt

(1913) 11 ALJ 489

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

A servant found his employer's missing nephew without knowing that the employer had announced a reward.

Issue

Whether performance without knowledge of an offer amounts to acceptance.

Holding

The claimant could not recover because the act was not performed in response to the reward offer; knowledge of the offer is necessary for acceptance of a unilateral proposal.

Why it matters

It supplies a clear fact pattern for communication, knowledge and acceptance.
