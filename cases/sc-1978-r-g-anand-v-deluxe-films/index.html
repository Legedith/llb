Supreme Court of India · 1978

R G Anand v Deluxe Films

(1978) 4 SCC 118

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

A playwright alleged that a film copied the theme and incidents of his play.

Issue

How are protectable expression and unprotectable ideas distinguished, and what constitutes substantial copying?

Holding

Copyright does not protect a general idea, theme or subject; infringement depends on substantial similarity in protected expression assessed through the overall impression and material similarities.

Why it matters

It is the foundational Indian copyright-infringement test.
