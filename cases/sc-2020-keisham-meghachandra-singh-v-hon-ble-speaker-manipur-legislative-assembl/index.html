Keisham Meghachandra Singh v Hon'ble Speaker, Manipur Legislative Assembly

(2020) 2 SCC 617

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

Anti-defection petitions remained undecided for a prolonged period while the disputed member continued in office.

Issue

Can courts require timely decision of defection petitions and grant interim protection?

Holding

The Court directed time-bound adjudication and suggested reconsideration of the Speaker's institutional role, reflecting concern about strategic delay.

Why it matters

It is a major current application of Kihoto Hollohan.
