Supreme Court of India · 2005

Jacob Mathew v State of Punjab

(2005) 6 SCC 1

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

A patient died after an oxygen cylinder was allegedly unavailable or empty, and the treating doctors faced criminal prosecution for negligence.

Issue

What degree of negligence justifies criminal liability of medical professionals, and how should courts assess professional standards?

Holding

Criminal negligence requires a very high degree of grossness; professional conduct is assessed against an ordinarily competent practitioner, and prosecution should not be launched casually.

Why it matters

It is the central Indian authority distinguishing civil medical negligence from criminal negligence.
