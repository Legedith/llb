Supreme Court of India · 1969

A K Kraipak v Union of India

(1969) 2 SCC 262

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

A selection-board member was himself a candidate for appointment, creating an institutional conflict in an administrative selection.

Issue

Do natural-justice rules apply to administrative action and what constitutes disqualifying bias?

Holding

The Court rejected a rigid administrative/quasi-judicial divide and applied fairness and bias principles to administrative decision-making.

Why it matters

It is the foundational Indian natural-justice and bias case.
