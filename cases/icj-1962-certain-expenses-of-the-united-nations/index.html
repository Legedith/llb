International Court of Justice · 1962

Certain Expenses of the United Nations

ICJ Reports 1962, p 151

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

The General Assembly requested advice on whether peacekeeping expenditures constituted expenses of the organisation binding on members.

Issue

How should the UN Charter allocate institutional functions and financial obligations?

Holding

The Court treated peacekeeping expenditures undertaken to fulfil UN purposes as organisational expenses, while distinguishing Security Council and General Assembly competences.

Why it matters

It is a key authority on institutional powers, Charter interpretation and UN finance.
