Supreme Court of India · 2013

Lalita Kumari v Government of Uttar Pradesh

[2013] 14 SCR 713

current with BNSS crosswalkDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

A minor's disappearance was reported but police allegedly delayed registration and investigation, leading to a larger reference on FIR duties.

Issue

Is registration of an FIR mandatory when information discloses a cognizable offence?

Holding

The Court held that registration is mandatory on disclosure of a cognizable offence, with a limited preliminary inquiry permitted in specified categories to determine whether such disclosure exists.

Why it matters

It is the foundational FIR-registration authority and must be read with current BNSS text.
