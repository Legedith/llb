International Court of Justice · 1969

North Sea Continental Shelf Cases

ICJ Reports 1969, p 3

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

Germany, Denmark and the Netherlands disputed continental-shelf boundaries and whether an equidistance rule bound Germany as custom.

Issue

How does a treaty rule become customary international law and what evidence proves custom?

Holding

The Court required extensive and virtually uniform state practice accompanied by opinio juris and rejected automatic binding force of the treaty rule on the non-party in the circumstances.

Why it matters

It is the canonical case on customary international law and maritime delimitation.
