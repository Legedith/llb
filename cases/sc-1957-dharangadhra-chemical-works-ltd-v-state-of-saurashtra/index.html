Supreme Court of India · 1957

Dharangadhra Chemical Works Ltd v State of Saurashtra

AIR 1957 SC 264

current with multifactor developmentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

Agarias working in salt production were described as independent contractors, but the actual degree of control and integration was disputed.

Issue

How is a contract of service distinguished from a contract for services?

Holding

The Court treated control and supervision as important but assessed the real relationship rather than contractual labels.

Why it matters

It is a foundational worker-status and misclassification case.
