Supreme Court of India · 2021

Tata Consultancy Services Ltd v Cyrus Investments Pvt Ltd

(2021) 9 SCC 449

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

Minority shareholders challenged the removal of Cyrus Mistry and broader governance decisions within the Tata group as oppression and mismanagement.

Issue

Whether board and shareholder decisions concerning removal and control amounted to oppressive conduct warranting tribunal intervention.

Holding

The Court restored the NCLT's dismissal, emphasising the statutory threshold for oppression and the limits of substituting judicial business judgment for lawful corporate governance.

Why it matters

It is a major contemporary authority on oppression, quasi-partnership claims and remedial limits.
