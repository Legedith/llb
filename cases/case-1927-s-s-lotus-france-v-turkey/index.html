S S Lotus (France v Turkey)

PCIJ Series A No 10

historical with treaty qualificationDiscovery link — verify
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

A collision on the high seas between French and Turkish vessels led Turkey to prosecute a French officer.

Issue

May a State exercise jurisdiction where international law contains no prohibitory rule?

Holding

The Court adopted a permissive approach: restrictions on state jurisdiction cannot simply be presumed, though later law and treaties qualify the decision's broad language.

Why it matters

It is a classic starting point for jurisdiction, sovereignty and the evolution of international law.
