Mohori Bibee v Dharmodas Ghose

[1903] UKPC 12

current with statutory qualificationsDiscovery link — verify
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

A minor mortgaged property to a moneylender whose agent knew of the minority; the minor later sought to avoid the transaction.

Issue

Whether an agreement by a minor is void or merely voidable under the Indian Contract Act.

Holding

An agreement with a minor is void from the beginning because a minor is not competent to contract; restitution cannot be used to enforce the void bargain indirectly.

Why it matters

It is the foundational capacity case and must be read with statutory rules on necessaries and restitution.
