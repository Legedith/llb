Supreme Court of India · 1984

Sharad Birdhichand Sarda v State of Maharashtra

[1985] 1 SCR 88

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

A husband was prosecuted for the poisoning death of his wife on a case resting largely on circumstantial evidence.

Issue

What safeguards govern conviction based solely on circumstantial evidence?

Holding

The Court stated the five linked conditions: circumstances must be fully established, consistent only with guilt, conclusive, exclude other hypotheses and form an unbroken chain.

Why it matters

It is the canonical circumstantial-evidence framework.
