Vidya Drolia v Durga Trading Corporation

(2021) 2 SCC 1

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

A tenancy dispute and arbitration clause required the Court to revisit non-arbitrability and the court's role at referral.

Issue

What disputes are non-arbitrable and how deeply should a referral court examine the issue?

Holding

The Court formulated a fourfold non-arbitrability test and adopted a restrained prima facie referral review, while treating certain rent disputes under special statutes differently from ordinary tenancy claims.

Why it matters

It is the present leading framework for arbitrability and referral review.
