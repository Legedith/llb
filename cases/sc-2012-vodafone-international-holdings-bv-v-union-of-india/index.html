Supreme Court of India · 2012

Vodafone International Holdings BV v Union of India

(2012) 6 SCC 613

statutorily superseded in partDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

An offshore transfer of shares in a foreign company indirectly shifted control over Indian assets; the revenue sought withholding tax.

Issue

Whether the pre-amendment income-tax law taxed the offshore share transfer because of its underlying Indian value.

Holding

The majority held that the transaction fell outside the then statutory charge and emphasised legal structure absent sham; Parliament later enacted retrospective amendments.

Why it matters

It is central to source rules, indirect transfers, corporate structure and the distinction between judicial interpretation and legislative response.
