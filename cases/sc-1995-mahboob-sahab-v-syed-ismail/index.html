Supreme Court of India · 1995

Mahboob Sahab v Syed Ismail

(1995) 3 SCC 693

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

A claimed Muslim gift of immovable property was disputed for want of proof of declaration, acceptance and delivery of possession.

Issue

What elements must be proved for a valid hiba?

Holding

The Court reiterated declaration by the donor, acceptance by the donee and delivery of possession, subject to the legal character of the property and transaction.

Why it matters

It is a frequently used Indian authority on the essentials of Muslim gifts.
