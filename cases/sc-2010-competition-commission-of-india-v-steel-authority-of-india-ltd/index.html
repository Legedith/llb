Supreme Court of India · 2010

Competition Commission of India v Steel Authority of India Ltd

(2010) 10 SCC 744

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

SAIL challenged a CCI direction ordering investigation and raised questions about hearing rights, appealability and the Commission's decisional stages.

Issue

Is a direction to investigate appealable and must the opposite party be heard before it is made?

Holding

The Court treated the prima facie investigation direction as administrative and non-appealable at that stage, while explaining CCI procedure and later hearing rights.

Why it matters

It is the foundational institutional-procedure case under the Competition Act.
