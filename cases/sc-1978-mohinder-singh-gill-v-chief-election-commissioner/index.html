Supreme Court of India · 1978

Mohinder Singh Gill v Chief Election Commissioner

(1978) 1 SCC 405

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

The Election Commission cancelled a poll after violence and malpractice, and its order was defended in court through reasons not contained in the original decision.

Issue

What is the scope of Article 324 and may an administrative order be supported by later reasons?

Holding

The Court recognised broad residual election-management power within law and stated that an order must stand or fall on the reasons contained in it, not later affidavits.

Why it matters

It is central to election administration and the general administrative-law rule against post-hoc reasons.
