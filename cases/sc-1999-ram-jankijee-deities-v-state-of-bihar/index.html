Supreme Court of India · 1999

Ram Jankijee Deities v State of Bihar

Citation requires verification

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

Property claimed for deities raised questions about the juristic entity, its representation and the distinction between idol and endowment.

Issue

How are deities represented and their property interests protected in law?

Holding

The juristic deity acts through a human representative, and courts distinguish the legal person, the physical idol and the manager who protects the endowed property.

Why it matters

It sharpens the institutional mechanics of non-natural legal personality.
