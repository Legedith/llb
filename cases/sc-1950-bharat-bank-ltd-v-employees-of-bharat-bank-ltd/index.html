Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

The status and functions of an industrial tribunal were examined in the constitutional setting.

Issue

What is the legal character of industrial adjudication?

Holding

Industrial tribunals perform adjudicatory functions affecting rights and obligations and possess many judicial characteristics, even though their constitutional classification and procedural flexibility differ from ordinary civil courts.

Why it matters

It explains why natural justice and judicial review matter in specialist labour adjudication.
