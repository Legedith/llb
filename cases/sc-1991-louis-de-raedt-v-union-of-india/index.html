Supreme Court of India · 1991

Louis De Raedt v Union of India

(1991) 3 SCC 554

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

Foreign nationals challenged decisions requiring them to leave India after permission to remain was withdrawn.

Issue

Do non-citizens possess a fundamental right to reside and settle in India?

Holding

The Court recognised Article 21 protection but held that the citizen-specific right to reside and settle does not extend to foreigners, whose stay remains governed by law.

Why it matters

It is an important boundary case for refugee and migration claims.
