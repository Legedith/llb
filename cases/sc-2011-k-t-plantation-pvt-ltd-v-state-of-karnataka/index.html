K T Plantation Pvt Ltd v State of Karnataka

Citation requires verification

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

A law affecting private property was challenged after the constitutional right to property ceased to be a fundamental right.

Issue

What protection does Article 300A provide against deprivation of property?

Holding

Deprivation of property requires authority of valid law; public purpose, non-arbitrariness and the character of compensation may be relevant to constitutional review, although Article 300A is not identical to the former fundamental right.

Why it matters

It connects ownership theory to the modern constitutional status of property.
