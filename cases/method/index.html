RESEARCH METHOD

How to read and cite a case

A case name is not a rule. Move from the legal problem to the exact proposition, then verify the source and later treatment.

1

Fix the proposition

Write the sentence the case is supposed to support. Do not collect citations without a legal job.

2

Read material facts

Identify facts that made the rule necessary and facts that limit its reach.

3

Separate ratio and order

Distinguish the binding reason, additional observations, separate opinions and the operative result.

4

Check authority

Verify court, bench strength, jurisdiction, procedural posture, citation and pinpoint.

5

Check later treatment

Search for affirming, distinguishing, limiting, overruling and statutory developments.

6

Apply, do not recite

Compare the legally material facts and explain why the proposition does or does not control.

Minimum safe citation note

Case + court/year + pinpoint + proposition + treatment. Add the material factual distinction when it matters.

The catalogue is a starting map. The judgment and current primary law remain controlling.
