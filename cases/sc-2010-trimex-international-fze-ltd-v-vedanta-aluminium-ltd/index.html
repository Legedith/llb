Trimex International FZE Ltd v Vedanta Aluminium Ltd

(2010) 3 SCC 1

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

Commercial parties negotiated the supply of bauxite through emails and later disputed whether a binding contract existed despite the absence of a formally signed document.

Issue

Can a concluded commercial contract arise from electronic correspondence even when a later formal document was contemplated?

Holding

The Court treated the essential terms and unconditional electronic acceptance as capable of creating a binding contract; contemplation of a formal document did not necessarily postpone formation.

Why it matters

It is a practical authority for email contracting and the difference between agreement and later documentation.
