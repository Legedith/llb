Supreme Court of India · 2015

Prakash v Phulavati

(2016) 2 SCC 36

overruledDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

A daughter claimed coparcenary rights under the 2005 amendment where her father had died before commencement.

Issue

Did the 2005 amendment require both daughter and father to be alive on commencement?

Holding

The decision adopted a prospective approach requiring the father to be alive. That proposition was later displaced by Vineeta Sharma.

Why it matters

It is important as an overruled step in the doctrinal trail.
