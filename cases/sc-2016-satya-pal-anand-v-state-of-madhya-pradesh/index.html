Supreme Court of India · 2016

Satya Pal Anand v State of Madhya Pradesh

(2016) 10 SCC 767

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

A registered deed was challenged before the registering authority after execution and registration.

Issue

Can a registrar cancel a completed registration on disputed title or validity grounds?

Holding

The Court held that the registration authority's powers are limited; substantive invalidity or cancellation generally requires proceedings before the competent civil court.

Why it matters

It is a practical authority on registration officers, title disputes and remedies.
