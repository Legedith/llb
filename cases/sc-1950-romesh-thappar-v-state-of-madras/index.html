Supreme Court of India · 1950

Romesh Thappar v State of Madras

AIR 1950 SC 124

current with amended articleDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

Madras prohibited circulation of a political journal on public-safety grounds.

Issue

How strictly must restrictions on press freedom fit the constitutional grounds in Article 19(2)?

Holding

The Court invalidated the restriction because the statutory ground did not fit the then constitutional limitation and treated circulation as part of freedom of speech.

Why it matters

It is an early foundational press-freedom and strict-grounds case.
