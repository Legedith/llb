Supreme Court of India · 2019

Pioneer Urban Land and Infrastructure Ltd v Govindan Raghavan

Citation requires verification

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

A homebuyer challenged one-sided terms and delay in delivery under a builder agreement.

Issue

Can a developer enforce oppressive standard-form terms against a consumer?

Holding

A dominant developer cannot rely on manifestly one-sided terms to defeat consumer relief; delay and unfair allocation of remedies may justify refund, interest and compensation under consumer law.

Why it matters

It links adhesion contracts, housing regulation and consumer remedies.
