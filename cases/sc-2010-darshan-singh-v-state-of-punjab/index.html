Supreme Court of India · 2010

Darshan Singh v State of Punjab

(2010) 2 SCC 333

current with code crosswalkDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

A fatal confrontation generated competing claims that force was used to defend person and property.

Issue

What principles govern commencement, proportionality and assessment of private defence?

Holding

The Court summarised that private defence is preventive, assessed realistically rather than with golden scales, but force must remain connected to a reasonable apprehension and statutory limits.

Why it matters

It is the modern synthesis of private-defence doctrine.
