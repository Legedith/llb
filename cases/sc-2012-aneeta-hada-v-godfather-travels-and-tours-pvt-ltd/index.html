Aneeta Hada v Godfather Travels and Tours Pvt Ltd

Citation requires verification

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

Directors were prosecuted for cheque dishonour without the company itself being properly arraigned as an accused.

Issue

Is prosecution of the company a condition for vicarious liability of officers under section 141 of the Negotiable Instruments Act?

Holding

Where liability is founded on the statutory vicarious-liability provision, arraigning and establishing the offence of the company is ordinarily a necessary condition before responsible officers can be held liable, subject to legally recognised exceptions such as impossibility.

Why it matters

It is the anchor for entity-first analysis in cheque-dishonour company cases.
