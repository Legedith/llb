Supreme Court of India · 2014

Anvar P V v P K Basheer

(2014) 10 SCC 473

current with clarificationDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

Electronic recordings were tendered in an election dispute without following the statutory certification route for secondary electronic evidence.

Issue

What formal conditions govern admissibility of electronic records produced through a copy or device output?

Holding

The Court required compliance with the electronic-record certificate provision for the relevant category of secondary electronic evidence and displaced contrary reasoning in State v Navjot Sandhu.

Why it matters

It reset Indian electronic-evidence doctrine and was later clarified by Arjun Panditrao.
