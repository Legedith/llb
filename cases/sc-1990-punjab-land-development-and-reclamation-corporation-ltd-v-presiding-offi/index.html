Supreme Court of India · 1990

Punjab Land Development and Reclamation Corporation Ltd v Presiding Officer, Labour Court

(1990) 3 SCC 682

current with code transitionDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

A Constitution Bench reviewed conflicting interpretations of retrenchment and statutory exclusions.

Issue

Does retrenchment cover every employer-initiated termination not expressly excluded?

Holding

The Court affirmed the broad statutory meaning and treated the listed exclusions as controlling limits.

Why it matters

It is the larger-bench authority consolidating the broad retrenchment rule.
