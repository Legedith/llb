Supreme Court of India · 2002

Central Bank of India v Ravindra

Citation requires verification

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

Banking claims raised questions about interest, capitalisation, rests and the extent to which unpaid interest can itself bear interest.

Issue

What principles govern capitalisation and award of interest in banking claims?

Holding

Interest may be capitalised only according to contract, banking practice and law; penal interest cannot ordinarily be capitalised in the same way, and courts must distinguish pre-suit, pendente lite and post-decree interest.

Why it matters

It is the leading conceptual map for interest calculations in banking litigation.
