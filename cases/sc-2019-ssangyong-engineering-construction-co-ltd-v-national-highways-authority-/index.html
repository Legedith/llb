Supreme Court of India · 2019

Ssangyong Engineering & Construction Co Ltd v National Highways Authority of India

[2019] 7 SCR 522

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

An arbitral tribunal used a government circular to alter the contractual price-adjustment formula, and the award was challenged after the 2015 amendments.

Issue

What is the post-amendment scope of public-policy and patent-illegality review of awards?

Holding

The Court narrowed public-policy review, explained patent illegality for domestic awards and set aside the relevant part because the tribunal effectively created a new bargain contrary to the contract and natural justice.

Why it matters

It is the leading post-2015 award-review authority.
