Supreme Court of India · 1969

Yogendra Nath Naskar v Commissioner of Income Tax

Citation requires verification

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

A Hindu deity's status and property were considered in a tax and endowment setting.

Issue

How does Indian law understand the legal personality of a Hindu idol or deity?

Holding

A consecrated deity may be recognised as a juristic person capable of holding property and being represented through a human manager, with legal personality serving the purposes of the endowment.

Why it matters

It is a foundational Indian example of functional juristic personality.
