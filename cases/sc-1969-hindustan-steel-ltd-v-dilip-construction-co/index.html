Supreme Court of India · 1969

Hindustan Steel Ltd v Dilip Construction Co

Citation requires verification

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

An instrument was insufficiently stamped and the issue concerned penalty and use after curing the defect.

Issue

What is the function of stamp law and when should penalty follow?

Holding

Stamp law secures revenue and provides a cure mechanism; penalty should be applied according to the statute and the character of the default rather than as an automatic weapon to defeat a genuine transaction.

Why it matters

It connects revenue protection, admissibility and proportional response to defects.
