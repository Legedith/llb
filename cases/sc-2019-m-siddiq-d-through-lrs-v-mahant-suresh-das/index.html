M Siddiq (D) through LRs v Mahant Suresh Das

Citation requires verification

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

Competing title claims over a religious site required analysis of possession, evidence, juristic personality, worship and remedies.

Issue

How do juristic personality, possessory evidence and title interact in a complex religious-property dispute?

Holding

The Court treated the deity as a juristic person, assessed documentary and archaeological evidence, distinguished faith from proof of title, and fashioned relief through the law of property and constitutional authority.

Why it matters

It is a rich case study in legal personality, evidence, possession and remedial design.
