Avinash Kumar Chauhan v Vijay Krishna Mishra

(2009) 2 SCC 532

current with NN Global distinctionDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

An insufficiently stamped instrument was tendered in civil proceedings and a party sought to rely on it for a collateral purpose.

Issue

May an unstamped instrument be admitted even for collateral use before duty and penalty are addressed?

Holding

The Court applied the strict statutory bar and required impounding and cure before admission.

Why it matters

It is a leading stamp-admissibility case, to be read with later arbitration-specific larger-bench law.
