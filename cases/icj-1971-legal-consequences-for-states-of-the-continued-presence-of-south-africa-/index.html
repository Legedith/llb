Legal Consequences for States of the Continued Presence of South Africa in Namibia

Citation requires verification

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

The Security Council had determined the illegality of South Africa's continued presence in Namibia and the General Assembly sought an advisory opinion on legal consequences.

Issue

What legal consequences followed from the termination of the mandate and relevant Security Council action?

Holding

South Africa's continued presence was illegal; states were obliged not to recognise or assist the unlawful situation while taking account of the interests of Namibia's people.

Why it matters

The opinion is central to Security Council authority, non-recognition and legal consequences of institutional decisions.
