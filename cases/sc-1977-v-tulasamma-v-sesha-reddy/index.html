Supreme Court of India · 1977

V Tulasamma v Sesha Reddy

(1977) 3 SCC 99

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

A Hindu widow received property in recognition of maintenance rights, and later transferees disputed whether she held it absolutely or as a restricted estate.

Issue

When does section 14(1) enlarge a woman's limited interest into absolute ownership, and when does section 14(2) preserve a restriction?

Holding

Property possessed in recognition of a pre-existing right is enlarged under section 14(1); section 14(2) is confined to a genuinely new restricted grant.

Why it matters

It is the foundational authority on enlargement of Hindu women's estates.
