Supreme Court of India · 2013

Lily Thomas v Union of India

(2013) 7 SCC 653

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

Petitioners challenged a statutory provision allowing sitting legislators to avoid immediate disqualification after conviction while an appeal was filed.

Issue

May sitting members receive a special grace period against constitutional disqualification on conviction?

Holding

The Court invalidated the protection, making disqualification operate from conviction subject to lawful appellate relief.

Why it matters

It is the principal authority on conviction-based disqualification of legislators.
