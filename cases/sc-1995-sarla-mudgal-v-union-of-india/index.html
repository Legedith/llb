Supreme Court of India · 1995

Sarla Mudgal v Union of India

(1995) 3 SCC 635

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

Hindu husbands converted to Islam and contracted second marriages without dissolving their first Hindu marriages.

Issue

Does conversion dissolve the first marriage or protect a second marriage from bigamy law?

Holding

Conversion does not automatically dissolve the subsisting marriage; a second marriage during its continuance can attract bigamy consequences.

Why it matters

It is the standard authority on conversion, bigamy and the continuing force of matrimonial status.
