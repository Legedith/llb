Supreme Court of India · 2014

Arnesh Kumar v State of Bihar

(2014) 8 SCC 273

current with BNSS crosswalkDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

An accused in a matrimonial-cruelty case challenged routine arrest practices in offences punishable below the statutory threshold.

Issue

How should police and magistrates apply statutory necessity conditions before arrest?

Holding

The Court directed police to justify arrest through statutory factors and magistrates to scrutinise detention rather than approve it mechanically.

Why it matters

It is the leading operational restriction on unnecessary arrest.
