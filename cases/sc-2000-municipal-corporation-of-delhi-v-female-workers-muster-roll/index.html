Supreme Court of India · 2000

Municipal Corporation of Delhi v Female Workers (Muster Roll)

(2000) 3 SCC 224

current with code crosswalkDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

Women engaged as muster-roll workers were denied maternity benefits available to regular employees.

Issue

Do maternity protections extend to casual or daily-wage workers?

Holding

The Court extended maternity benefit on constitutional, statutory and social-justice grounds, rejecting status-based denial.

Why it matters

It is a leading labour and gender-equality authority on care work and informal employment.
