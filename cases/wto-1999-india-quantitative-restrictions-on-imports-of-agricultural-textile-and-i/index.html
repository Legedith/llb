India — Quantitative Restrictions on Imports of Agricultural, Textile and Industrial Products

WT/DS90

historical compliedDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

The United States challenged India's import restrictions maintained on balance-of-payments grounds.

Issue

Could India continue broad quantitative restrictions under the GATT balance-of-payments exception?

Holding

The panels and Appellate Body found that the restrictions were not justified under the claimed exception and required their removal.

Why it matters

It is a principal India-specific case on quantitative restrictions, exceptions and WTO review.
