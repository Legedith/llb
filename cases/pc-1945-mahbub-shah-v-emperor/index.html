Mahbub Shah v Emperor

AIR 1945 PC 118

current with later developmentDiscovery link — verify
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

Members of a group were present during a violent incident, but the evidence of a pre-arranged plan was disputed.

Issue

What proof is required for constructive liability based on common intention?

Holding

Common intention requires a pre-arranged plan or meeting of minds inferred from proved circumstances; mere presence or similar intention is insufficient.

Why it matters

It is the classic caution against mechanically imposing joint liability.
