Supreme Court of India · 1994

Tata Cellular v Union of India

(1994) 6 SCC 651

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

Competing telecom bidders challenged government evaluation and award of licences.

Issue

What is the proper scope of judicial review over commercial tender decisions?

Holding

The Court confined review to legality, fairness, rationality and absence of mala fides, not substitution of judicial views on commercial merits.

Why it matters

It is the leading Indian authority on tender review and administrative restraint.
