International Criminal Court · 2012

Prosecutor v Thomas Lubanga Dyilo

ICC-01/04-01/06

currentDiscovery link — verify
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

A Congolese militia leader was prosecuted for conscripting, enlisting and using children under fifteen in hostilities.

Issue

What elements establish the war crime of recruiting and using child soldiers?

Holding

The ICC convicted Lubanga and later developed victim-participation and reparation principles, while debate continued about charging strategy and sexual violence evidence.

Why it matters

It is the ICC's first trial judgment and a practical study of child-soldier offences and reparations.
