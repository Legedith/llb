Reserve Bank of India v Peerless General Finance and Investment Co Ltd

Citation requires verification

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

A regulatory statute and scheme had to be interpreted in their full textual and institutional setting.

Issue

How should statutory text be read in context?

Holding

Interpretation depends on the text and context together; a provision should be read as part of the entire statute, its scheme and purpose rather than as an isolated sentence.

Why it matters

It is a concise anchor for whole-statute and contextual interpretation.
