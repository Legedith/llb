Balwant Singh v Jagdish Singh

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

An application sought condonation or procedural relief after delay and default.

Issue

What does sufficient cause require in limitation and delay-condonation analysis?

Holding

Courts may adopt a justice-oriented approach, but sufficient cause still requires a bona fide, reasonable and adequately explained account; limitation cannot be displaced by sympathy or vague assertions.

Why it matters

The case is useful for distinguishing liberality from absence of standards.
