Alcon Electronics Pvt Ltd v Celem SA

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

A foreign court order for costs was sought to be recognised and executed in India.

Issue

When is a foreign judgment conclusive and executable under the CPC?

Holding

A judgment from a competent foreign court may be conclusive and executable when it is on merits and does not fall within the statutory exceptions concerning natural justice, fraud, incorrect jurisdictional foundation or breach of Indian law.

Why it matters

The case provides a practical foreign-judgment enforcement example beyond matrimonial litigation.
