Samar Ghosh v Jaya Ghosh

(2007) 4 SCC 511

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

Long-running matrimonial litigation required assessment of conduct alleged to amount to mental cruelty.

Issue

How should mental cruelty be identified without reducing it to a rigid formula?

Holding

The Court offered illustrative, non-exhaustive categories and stressed cumulative context, relationship history and the gravity of conduct.

Why it matters

It is the most-used Indian guide to mental cruelty, but its illustrations are not a checklist that replaces facts.
