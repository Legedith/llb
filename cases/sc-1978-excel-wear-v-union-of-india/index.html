Supreme Court of India · 1978

Excel Wear v Union of India

(1978) 4 SCC 224

historical with legislationDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

Employers challenged a law requiring prior government permission to close an undertaking.

Issue

How should labour protection be balanced with the constitutional freedom to close a business?

Holding

The Court invalidated the then provision as excessively restrictive, while accepting that closure may be regulated through a constitutionally proportionate scheme.

Why it matters

It is the leading case on closure, business freedom and labour regulation.
