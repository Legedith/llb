Supreme Court of India · 1999

Shiv Sarup Gupta v Dr Mahesh Chand Gupta

Citation requires verification

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

A landlord sought eviction on the ground of bona fide personal requirement and the tenant disputed need and alternative accommodation.

Issue

How should bona fide requirement and suitable alternative accommodation be assessed?

Holding

The court examines the landlord's genuine, honest need from a practical perspective while testing objective facts, the nature of the premises and reasonably suitable alternative accommodation; the tenant cannot dictate the landlord's lifestyle, but the claim is not immune from scrutiny.

Why it matters

It supplies the core factual method for landlord-need cases.
