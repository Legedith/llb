Rupa Ashok Hurra v Ashok Hurra

Citation requires verification

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

After dismissal of a review petition, a litigant alleged a grave failure of natural justice in a final Supreme Court judgment.

Issue

Does the Supreme Court possess a narrow curative jurisdiction after review?

Holding

To prevent a gross miscarriage of justice in exceptional circumstances, the Court recognised a tightly confined curative petition following review, subject to certification, circulation and demanding threshold conditions.

Why it matters

It is the foundation of curative jurisdiction and finality safeguards.
