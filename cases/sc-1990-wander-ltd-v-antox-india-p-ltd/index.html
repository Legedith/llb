Wander Ltd v Antox India P Ltd

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

An appellate court reviewed a discretionary interlocutory injunction granted or refused by the court of first instance.

Issue

When may an appellate court interfere with a discretionary interim-injunction order?

Holding

Appellate interference is limited where the first court exercised discretion judicially on relevant principles; a different possible view is not enough unless the order is arbitrary, capricious, perverse or legally mistaken.

Why it matters

It separates the three injunction factors from the distinct standard governing appellate review of discretion.
