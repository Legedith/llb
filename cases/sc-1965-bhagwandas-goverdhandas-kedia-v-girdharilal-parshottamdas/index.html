Bhagwandas Goverdhandas Kedia v Girdharilal Parshottamdas

AIR 1966 SC 543

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

Parties negotiated a contract by long-distance telephone and disputed where the contract was made for jurisdictional purposes.

Issue

Where is a contract concluded when acceptance is communicated through an instantaneous method such as telephone?

Holding

For instantaneous communications, the contract is made where acceptance is received and heard, unlike the traditional postal rule.

Why it matters

It is the Indian anchor for instantaneous acceptance and territorial jurisdiction in communications contracts.
