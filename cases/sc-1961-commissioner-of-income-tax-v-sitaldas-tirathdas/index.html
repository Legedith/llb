Supreme Court of India · 1961

Commissioner of Income Tax v Sitaldas Tirathdas

(1961) 2 SCR 634

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

An assessee paid amounts to family members under an obligation and claimed that the income had been diverted before reaching him.

Issue

How is diversion of income by overriding title distinguished from application of income after receipt?

Holding

Income is excluded only where an overriding obligation diverts it at source; a personal obligation to apply income after it accrues does not prevent taxation.

Why it matters

It is the foundational income-diversion test.
