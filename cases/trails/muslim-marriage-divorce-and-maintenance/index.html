DEVELOPMENT TRAIL

Muslim marriage, divorce and post-divorce maintenance

The interaction of personal law, statutory interpretation, constitutional equality and maintenance remedies.

1
1985 · Supreme Court of India

Mohd Ahmed Khan v Shah Bano Begum

The Court held that the remedy applied irrespective of religion and that the husband's personal-law obligations did not exclude the statutory duty.

2
2001 · Supreme Court of India

Danial Latifi v Union of India

The Court upheld the Act by construing it to require a fair provision made within iddat that can meet the divorced woman's future needs beyond that period.

3
2002 · Supreme Court of India

Shamim Ara v State of Uttar Pradesh

A mere assertion in a written statement is insufficient; talaq must be proved as pronounced for reasonable cause and preceded by an attempt at reconciliation in the framework adopted by the Court.

4
2017 · Supreme Court of India

Shayara Bano v Union of India

A majority set aside talaq-e-biddat, though the judges used different routes, including arbitrariness and the status of the practice within personal law.
