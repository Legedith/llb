Patents, pharmaceuticals and public-interest limits

How patentability, incremental innovation and infringement remedies interact with access and competition.

1
2013 · Supreme Court of India

Novartis AG v Union of India

The Court rejected the claim because the evidence did not establish enhanced therapeutic efficacy over the known substance and carefully distinguished patentability from general improvement.

2
2015 · Delhi High Court

F Hoffmann-La Roche Ltd v Cipla Ltd

The appellate judgment applied purposive claim construction and analysed infringement separately from validity, while addressing obviousness and public-interest arguments.
