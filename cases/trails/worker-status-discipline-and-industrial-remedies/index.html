DEVELOPMENT TRAIL

Worker status, discipline and industrial remedies

A trail from employment classification and industry definition to domestic inquiry, tribunal review and remedial restoration.

1
1957 · Supreme Court of India

Dharangadhra Chemical Works Ltd v State of Saurashtra

The Court treated control and supervision as important but assessed the real relationship rather than contractual labels.

2
1978 · Supreme Court of India

Bangalore Water Supply and Sewerage Board v A Rajappa

The Court formulated the triple test of systematic activity, cooperation between employer and employee and production or distribution of goods or services for human wants, subject to limited sovereign-function exclusion.

3
1973 · Supreme Court of India

Workmen of Firestone Tyre & Rubber Co of India v Management

The Court explained when a tribunal may reappraise evidence, permit the employer to prove misconduct and examine punishment, principles later reflected in section 11A.

4
1979 · Supreme Court of India

Hindustan Tin Works Pvt Ltd v Employees

Reinstatement may ordinarily carry restoration of lost wages, but relief depends on the facts, including the nature of illegality, delay, gainful employment and equitable considerations; later law has made the inquiry more contextual.

5
2002 · Supreme Court of India

Jaipur Zila Sahakari Bhoomi Vikas Bank Ltd v Ram Gopal Sharma

Where statutory approval is required and refused or not obtained, the dismissal does not become legally effective in the manner contemplated by the provision, protecting the worker during pending proceedings.
