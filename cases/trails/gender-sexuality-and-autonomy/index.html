DEVELOPMENT TRAIL

Gender, sexuality and decisional autonomy

A trail connecting workplace equality, gender identity, sexual orientation, marriage, bodily autonomy and reproductive choice.

1
1997 · Supreme Court of India

Vishaka v State of Rajasthan

The Court held that workplace sexual harassment violates equality, dignity, liberty and occupational freedom, used international norms consistent with fundamental rights, and issued binding guidelines until legislation was enacted.

2
2014 · Supreme Court of India

National Legal Services Authority v Union of India

The Court recognised transgender persons as entitled to constitutional equality, dignity and self-identification, and directed affirmative governmental measures.

3
2018 · Supreme Court of India

Navtej Singh Johar v Union of India

The Court invalidated section 377 to the extent it criminalised consensual adult intimacy, while leaving non-consensual acts and acts involving minors outside that protection.

4
2018 · Supreme Court of India

Joseph Shine v Union of India

The Court struck down the offence as arbitrary, discriminatory and incompatible with dignity and sexual autonomy, while clarifying that adultery may remain relevant in civil matrimonial law.

5
2017 · Supreme Court of India

Independent Thought v Union of India

The Court read down the exception so that intercourse with a wife below eighteen falls within the rape offence, harmonising criminal, child-protection and marriage laws.

6
2022 · Supreme Court of India

X v Principal Secretary, Health and Family Welfare Department, Government of NCT of Delhi

The Court interpreted the law inclusively, rejected marital-status discrimination and connected reproductive choice with dignity, privacy and changing family forms.
