DEVELOPMENT TRAIL

Copyright: expression, originality and user rights

The development of idea-expression separation, originality, educational use and moral rights.

1
1978 · Supreme Court of India

R G Anand v Deluxe Films

Copyright does not protect a general idea, theme or subject; infringement depends on substantial similarity in protected expression assessed through the overall impression and material similarities.

2
2008 · Supreme Court of India

Eastern Book Company v D B Modak

The Court rejected pure sweat of the brow and required creativity in the selection or arrangement, while recognising protection for qualifying editorial contributions but not the underlying judgments.

3
2016 · Delhi High Court

The Chancellor, Masters & Scholars of the University of Oxford v Rameshwari Photocopy Services

The Division Bench adopted a broad reading of use in the course of instruction and rejected infringement on the facts, while emphasising the educational setting.

4
2005 · Delhi High Court

Amar Nath Sehgal v Union of India

The Court recognised strong moral-right protection against distortion, mutilation and destruction prejudicial to the artist's honour and reputation.
