DEVELOPMENT TRAIL

Substantive equality and reservation doctrine

The development of backward-class identification, creamy layer, promotion, economic criteria and sub-classification.

1
1992 · Supreme Court of India

Indra Sawhney v Union of India

The Court upheld OBC reservation, required exclusion of the creamy layer, generally limited reservation to fifty per cent and rejected reservation in promotion under the then text.

2
2018 · Supreme Court of India

Jarnail Singh v Lachhmi Narain Gupta

The Court removed the requirement to collect data proving backwardness of SCs and STs, while retaining other constitutional requirements and applying the creamy-layer principle to exclude the advanced.

3
2022 · Supreme Court of India

Janhit Abhiyan v Union of India

A majority upheld the amendment; the dissent disagreed particularly with exclusion. The decision accepts economic criteria for the new constitutional category.

4
2024 · Supreme Court of India

State of Punjab v Davinder Singh

The Court held that sub-classification is permissible when supported by relevant data and directed toward more effective distribution of benefits; individual opinions differed on aspects such as creamy layer.
