DEVELOPMENT TRAIL

Basic structure and limited constitutional amendment

How an initially broad amendment power became subject to judicially enforceable limits protecting constitutional identity.

1
1973 · Supreme Court of India

His Holiness Kesavananda Bharati Sripadagalvaru v State of Kerala

Parliament's amending power is wide but cannot damage or destroy the Constitution's basic structure. The opinions differed on detail, but the basic-structure limitation became the controlling proposition.

2
1975 · Supreme Court of India

Indira Nehru Gandhi v Raj Narain

The Court invalidated the immunity provision through basic-structure reasoning, emphasising free and fair elections, equality and judicial review.

3
1980 · Supreme Court of India

Minerva Mills Ltd v Union of India

The Court held that limited amending power and the harmony between fundamental rights and Directive Principles are features of the basic structure.

4
2007 · Supreme Court of India

I R Coelho v State of Tamil Nadu

The Court held that post-24 April 1973 Ninth Schedule laws remain reviewable where their impact damages basic-structure principles reflected in fundamental rights.

5
2022 · Supreme Court of India

Janhit Abhiyan v Union of India

A majority upheld the amendment; the dissent disagreed particularly with exclusion. The decision accepts economic criteria for the new constitutional category.
