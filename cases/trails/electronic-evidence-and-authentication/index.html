DEVELOPMENT TRAIL

Electronic evidence, authentication and source integrity

How Indian evidence law moved toward mandatory electronic-record certification while confronting authenticity and fabricated authority.

1
2014 · Supreme Court of India

Anvar P V v P K Basheer

The Court required compliance with the electronic-record certificate provision for the relevant category of secondary electronic evidence and displaced contrary reasoning in State v Navjot Sandhu.

2
2020 · Supreme Court of India

Arjun Panditrao Khotkar v Kailash Kushanrao Gorantyal

The Court reaffirmed the certificate requirement for secondary electronic evidence, clarified that it may be produced at an appropriate stage and recognised court assistance where the record is controlled by another person.

3
2026 · Supreme Court of India

Pooja Ramesh Singh v Jammu and Kashmir Bank Ltd

The Court set aside the tainted decisions, held that a decision infected by fabricated precedent cannot stand, adopted zero tolerance for unverified hallucinated authority and directed the Bar Council of India to consider professional standards.
