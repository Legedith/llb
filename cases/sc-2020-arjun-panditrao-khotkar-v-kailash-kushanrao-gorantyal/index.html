Arjun Panditrao Khotkar v Kailash Kushanrao Gorantyal

(2020) 7 SCC 1

current with BSA crosswalkDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

Election litigation raised renewed questions about electronic records and the availability and timing of the statutory certificate.

Issue

Is the certificate requirement mandatory and what happens when the party does not control the relevant device?

Holding

The Court reaffirmed the certificate requirement for secondary electronic evidence, clarified that it may be produced at an appropriate stage and recognised court assistance where the record is controlled by another person.

Why it matters

It is the present leading authority on electronic-record certification under the predecessor statute, requiring crosswalk to the BSA.
