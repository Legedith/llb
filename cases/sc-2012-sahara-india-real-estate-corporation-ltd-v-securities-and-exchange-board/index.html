Supreme Court of India · 2012

Sahara India Real Estate Corporation Ltd v Securities and Exchange Board of India

Citation requires verification

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

Group companies raised large sums through instruments offered to numerous investors and disputed SEBI's jurisdiction and the public character of the issue.

Issue

When does a purported private placement become a public issue subject to securities regulation?

Holding

Substance, number and manner of offerees, disclosure and statutory thresholds determine whether an issue is public; labels and corporate structuring cannot evade investor-protection duties and SEBI jurisdiction.

Why it matters

It is a major case study in regulatory perimeter, disclosure and restitution.
