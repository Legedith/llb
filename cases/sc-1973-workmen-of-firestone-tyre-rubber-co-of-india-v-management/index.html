Supreme Court of India · 1973

Workmen of Firestone Tyre & Rubber Co of India v Management

(1973) 1 SCC 813

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

Industrial adjudication disputes required the Court to define the consequences of a fair, defective or absent domestic inquiry.

Issue

What powers does an industrial tribunal have over dismissal and disciplinary findings?

Holding

The Court explained when a tribunal may reappraise evidence, permit the employer to prove misconduct and examine punishment, principles later reflected in section 11A.

Why it matters

It is the foundational map for domestic inquiry and dismissal review.
