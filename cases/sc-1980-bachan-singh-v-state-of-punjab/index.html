Supreme Court of India · 1980

Bachan Singh v State of Punjab

(1980) 2 SCC 684

current with later developmentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

A death sentence challenge required the Court to examine the constitutional validity and sentencing standards for capital punishment.

Issue

When is the death penalty constitutionally permissible?

Holding

The Court upheld the penalty but confined it to the rarest of rare cases after individualised consideration of crime and criminal, with life imprisonment as the normal rule.

Why it matters

It is the foundational capital-sentencing authority.
