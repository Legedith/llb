Pooja Ramesh Singh v Jammu and Kashmir Bank Ltd

2026 INSC 668

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

NCLT and NCLAT orders in insolvency proceedings reproduced authorities and paragraphs that were discovered to be fabricated or falsely attributed, apparently through unverified AI-assisted research.

Issue

What is the legal consequence when an adjudicatory decision relies on fake or hallucinated precedents, and what professional controls are required?

Holding

The Court set aside the tainted decisions, held that a decision infected by fabricated precedent cannot stand, adopted zero tolerance for unverified hallucinated authority and directed the Bar Council of India to consider professional standards.

Why it matters

It is a crucial current case for legal research, citation verification, judicial reasoning and professional responsibility.
