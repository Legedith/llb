Supreme Court of India · 1966

Municipal Corporation of Delhi v Subhagwanti

AIR 1966 SC 1750

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

An old clock tower collapsed in a crowded area, killing and injuring people; the municipal authority controlled the structure.

Issue

Could negligence be inferred where a structure under the defendant's control collapsed in the ordinary course?

Holding

The Court applied res ipsa loquitur and held the authority liable because the occurrence itself strongly indicated lack of proper care.

Why it matters

It is the standard Indian illustration of res ipsa loquitur and public-authority negligence.
