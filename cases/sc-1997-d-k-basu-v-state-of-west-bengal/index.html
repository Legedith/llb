Supreme Court of India · 1997

D K Basu v State of West Bengal

[1996] Supp 10 SCR 284

current with code crosswalkDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

Letters and petitions concerning custodial deaths and torture were treated as public-interest litigation.

Issue

What safeguards must govern arrest and detention to reduce custodial abuse and make responsibility traceable?

Holding

The Court prescribed arrest, documentation, notification, medical examination and production safeguards, treating non-compliance as actionable and potentially contemptuous.

Why it matters

It is the operational constitutional authority on arrest safeguards and custodial accountability.
