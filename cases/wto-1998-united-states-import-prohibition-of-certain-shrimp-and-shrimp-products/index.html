United States — Import Prohibition of Certain Shrimp and Shrimp Products

WT/DS58

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

The United States restricted shrimp imports based on sea-turtle protection methods applied by exporting countries.

Issue

How do environmental trade restrictions fit within GATT Article XX and its chapeau?

Holding

The measure pursued a permitted environmental objective but its discriminatory application initially failed the chapeau; later compliance focused on flexibility and good-faith negotiation.

Why it matters

It is the classic WTO case on environmental exceptions, process regulation and non-discrimination.
