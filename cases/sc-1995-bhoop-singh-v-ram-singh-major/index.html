Supreme Court of India · 1995

Bhoop Singh v Ram Singh Major

Citation requires verification

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

A compromise decree concerning immovable property raised whether the decree itself required registration.

Issue

When does a compromise decree require registration?

Holding

The statutory exemption for decrees does not permit parties to use a consent decree as a device for creating rights in property outside the subject matter of the suit without registration.

Why it matters

It explains the boundary between exempt decrees and registrable compromise arrangements.
