Supreme Court of India · 2013

Novartis AG v Union of India

[2013] 13 SCR 148

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

Novartis sought a patent for the beta-crystalline form of imatinib mesylate used in a cancer medicine.

Issue

Did the claimed form satisfy invention and patentability requirements, including the enhanced-efficacy test in section 3(d)?

Holding

The Court rejected the claim because the evidence did not establish enhanced therapeutic efficacy over the known substance and carefully distinguished patentability from general improvement.

Why it matters

It is the landmark Indian pharmaceutical-patent and anti-evergreening case.
