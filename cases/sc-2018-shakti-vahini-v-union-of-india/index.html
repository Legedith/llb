Supreme Court of India · 2018

Shakti Vahini v Union of India

[2018] 3 SCR 770

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

Public-interest litigation addressed honour crimes and interference by caste or community assemblies with inter-caste and inter-faith marriages.

Issue

What preventive and remedial duties does the State owe to adults choosing their partners?

Holding

The Court affirmed the right to choose a partner and issued preventive, protective and punitive directions against honour-based interference.

Why it matters

It links constitutional autonomy with policing, district administration and victim protection.
