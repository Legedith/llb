Supreme Court of India · 1996

Delhi Development Authority v Skipper Construction Co

(1996) 4 SCC 622

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

Promoters used corporate entities and property transactions to defraud purchasers and defeat court orders.

Issue

May the corporate veil be lifted where companies are used as devices for fraud?

Holding

The Court disregarded the corporate structure to prevent fraud and enforce restitutionary and protective orders.

Why it matters

It is the practical fraud-based counterpart to the cautious approach in LIC v Escorts.

Application · LB-302Recall of orders obtained by fraud

Courts may fashion orders to prevent abuse and defeat fraudulent structures.
