Supreme Court of India · 2006

Prakash Singh v Union of India

(2006) 8 SCC 1

current poorly implementedDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

Former police leaders sought structural reforms to reduce political control, insecure tenure and weak accountability in policing.

Issue

What institutional safeguards are necessary for professional and accountable policing?

Holding

The Court directed States and the Union to establish security commissions, merit-based selection, minimum tenure, separation of investigation and police complaints authorities.

Why it matters

It is the central police-reform authority, notable also for persistent implementation gaps.
