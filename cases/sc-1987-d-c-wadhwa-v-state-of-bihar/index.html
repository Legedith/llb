Supreme Court of India · 1987

D C Wadhwa v State of Bihar

(1987) 1 SCC 378

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

A researcher challenged Bihar's longstanding practice of keeping policies alive through successive ordinances rather than legislation.

Issue

Whether repeated re-promulgation may be used to bypass the legislature.

Holding

The Court held that re-promulgation subverts the constitutional scheme and cannot become a substitute for legislative enactment.

Why it matters

It is the foundational ordinance-abuse case later developed by Krishna Kumar Singh.
