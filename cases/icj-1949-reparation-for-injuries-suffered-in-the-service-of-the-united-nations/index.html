International Court of Justice · 1949

Reparation for Injuries Suffered in the Service of the United Nations

ICJ Reports 1949, p 174

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

A UN mediator was assassinated and the General Assembly asked whether the organisation could bring an international claim.

Issue

Does the United Nations possess international legal personality and implied powers to protect its agents?

Holding

The Court recognised objective international personality and powers necessary for the organisation to perform its functions.

Why it matters

It is the foundational authority on international-organisation personality and implied powers.
