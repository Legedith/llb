K B Saha and Sons Pvt Ltd v Development Consultant Ltd

Citation requires verification

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

An unregistered document affecting immovable property was offered in evidence for a claimed collateral purpose.

Issue

When may an unregistered document be used for a collateral purpose?

Holding

An unregistered instrument required to be registered cannot prove the transaction affecting immovable property, but may be admitted for a genuinely collateral matter independent of the transaction that required registration.

Why it matters

It supplies a practical test for the often-misused collateral-purpose exception.
