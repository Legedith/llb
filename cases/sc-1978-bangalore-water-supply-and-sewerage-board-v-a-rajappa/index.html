Supreme Court of India · 1978

Bangalore Water Supply and Sewerage Board v A Rajappa

(1978) 2 SCC 213

current with legislative transitionDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

Employees of a statutory water-supply body raised an industrial dispute and the employer argued that its activity was not an 'industry'.

Issue

What activities fall within the broad statutory concept of industry?

Holding

The Court formulated the triple test of systematic activity, cooperation between employer and employee and production or distribution of goods or services for human wants, subject to limited sovereign-function exclusion.

Why it matters

It remains the foundational industry-definition case, though later legislation and commencement questions must be checked.
