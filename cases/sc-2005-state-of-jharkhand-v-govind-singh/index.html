Supreme Court of India · 2005

State of Jharkhand v Govind Singh

Citation requires verification

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

A penal provision was sought to be applied beyond the conduct expressly covered by its words.

Issue

Can a court expand a statute to address an apparent omission or policy concern?

Holding

Judicial interpretation cannot add words or create an offence that the legislature did not enact; an apparent gap must ordinarily be left for legislative correction.

Why it matters

It demonstrates the separation-of-powers reason behind the casus-omissus rule.
