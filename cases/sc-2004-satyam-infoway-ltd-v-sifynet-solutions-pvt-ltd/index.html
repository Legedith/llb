Supreme Court of India · 2004

Satyam Infoway Ltd v Sifynet Solutions Pvt Ltd

(2004) 6 SCC 145

currentDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

Competing internet businesses used confusingly similar domain names and one sought passing-off relief.

Issue

Can a domain name function as a protected business identifier under passing-off law?

Holding

The Court held that domain names can acquire goodwill and source significance and may be protected against deceptive similarity.

Why it matters

It is the foundational Indian domain-name case.
