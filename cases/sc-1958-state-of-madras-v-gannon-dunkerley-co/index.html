Supreme Court of India · 1958

State of Madras v Gannon Dunkerley & Co

AIR 1958 SC 560

statutorily superseded in partDirect source link
Use the holding, not the headline

This is an educational synthesis. Verify the judgment text, material facts, ratio, operative order, bench strength and later treatment before citing it.

Facts

A building contractor was assessed to sales tax as though materials transferred under an indivisible works contract constituted a conventional sale of goods.

Issue

Did the constitutional and statutory concept of sale include the material component of an indivisible works contract under the then law?

Holding

The Court held that the traditional sale concept required an agreement to sell goods and transfer of property as goods; Parliament later altered the constitutional framework for works contracts.

Why it matters

It is a classic example of sale classification, legislative response and constitutional amendment.
